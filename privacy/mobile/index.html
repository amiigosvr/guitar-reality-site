This Privacy Policy describes how AmiigosVR handles information in connection with Guitar Reality for phones and tablets. Playing on Meta Quest? Read the Meta Quest privacy policy.

01

Information stored on your device

Guitar Reality stores gameplay information locally on your phone or tablet. This includes settings, touch and controller layouts, timing calibration, progress, fans, unlocks, achievements, high scores and song-library information. It is used to keep your preferences, show your progress and run game features.

Songs you import are copied into the game's own storage on your device and are used only to scan, display and play them. They are never uploaded to AmiigosVR. Diagnostic logs the game writes also stay on your device unless you choose to send one to us.

Touch, gamepad, guitar-controller and motion input is processed on the device to run the game. Guitar Reality does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, photos or precise location.

02

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Guitar Reality shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner in the menus, occasional full-screen ads between songs, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch. To serve and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google your IP address (used to estimate your approximate location), device and advertising identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, your interactions with ads and the app, and diagnostic information about app and SDK performance. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and it is encrypted in transit.

Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears before ads load. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy options when that option is shown. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google or privacy settings.

On iPhone and iPad, Guitar Reality does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to AdMob and ads are not targeted by tracking you across other companies' apps.

AmiigosVR receives only aggregated ad reports, such as impressions and earnings, and never your identity. Google processes ad data under the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

03

Remove Ads purchase

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. AmiigosVR does not receive your payment card details, billing address or store account email. The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing, by Unity Technologies, to ask the store whether your account owns the purchase so it can turn ads off and restore the purchase later. Unity may process technical information needed for this under the Unity Privacy Policy.

04

Sign-in, leaderboards and cloud save

Android: Guitar Reality uses Google Play Games Services. When you are signed in to Play Games, Google shares your gamer name, avatar and player ID with the game; scores you post appear on leaderboards under that profile, visible according to your Play Games profile settings; and your progress (fans, scores, achievements, unlocks and tutorial progress) is backed up as Saved Games in your Google account. Google also collects Play Games analytics and diagnostics under its own privacy policy.

iPhone and iPad: Guitar Reality uses Game Center for leaderboards, under your Game Center nickname, and iCloud key-value storage to back up the same progress in your Apple account.

AmiigosVR runs no servers for these features and does not receive your email address. Our developer consoles show leaderboard entries by gamer name only. If you don't sign in, your progress stays only on your device.

05

Analytics, crash reports and sharing

Guitar Reality does not use developer-operated analytics, cloud crash-reporting or tracking services beyond the Google and Apple services described above, and AmiigosVR does not sell personal information. Google Play and Apple may provide us with aggregated crash and performance statistics from people who allowed sharing them in their device settings; these do not identify you.

06

Beta testing

TestFlight (iPhone and iPad): Apple shares with us the feedback, screenshots and crash reports you choose to send through TestFlight, together with technical details such as your device model and system version, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms.

Google Play testing (Android): you join the test through our Google Group, and as its managers we can see members' email addresses. We use that list only to run the test and send test updates, and you can leave the group at any time.

Beta versions show Google's test ads and work the same way as described in this policy. We use beta feedback only to improve Guitar Reality.

07

Support communications

If you contact AmiigosVR by email, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, and any diagnostic information you attach. We use it only to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues, protect our rights, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell support information.

08

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored information stays on your device until you delete it. On Android, clear Guitar Reality's storage in your phone's app settings or uninstall the game. On iPhone and iPad, delete the app to remove its data and imported songs.

Play Games data, including Saved Games, can be deleted from your Play Games profile or Google Account. Game Center and iCloud data is managed in your Apple Account settings. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own retention policies, and you can reset your Android advertising ID at any time.

To request deletion of information you provided through a support communication or the testers group, email amiigosvr@gmail.com. We will delete it unless retention is required for security, legal, or regulatory reasons.

09

Children's privacy

Guitar Reality is not directed to children under 13. AmiigosVR does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and delete it.

10

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Guitar Reality's features or data practices change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the effective date.

11

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact AmiigosVR at amiigosvr@gmail.com.
